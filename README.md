# പെരുവള്ളൂർ കപ്പ് Season 7 (Peruvallur Cup 2026)
### Official All-India Sevens Football Tournament Web Portal

Jointly Organized by **KASC Peruvallur** & **Udaya Parambil Peedika**  
Affiliated with **Sevens Football Association (SFA)**

---

## 🌟 Overview
An electrifying, mobile-first live tournament portal engineered for local fans in Malappuram and thousands of Malayali expatriates across the Gulf (UAE, Saudi Arabia, Qatar, Oman, Bahrain, Kuwait).

### Key Features
1. **Live Match Center:**
   - Real-time score ticker, live minute counter, and event timeline.
   - Interactive goal celebrations with confetti effects.
2. **Opening Blockbuster Showcase:**
   - **BASE Peruvallur** vs **SABAN FC Kottakkal** (Kickoff: Friday, 06 Nov 2026 at 8:00 PM).
3. **Official Fixtures & Schedule:**
   - Knockout fixtures from Round 1 through the Grand Final.
4. **Golden Boot Leaderboard:**
   - Live top goalscorers tracking Indian and international stars.
5. **Interactive Scorer Demo Modal:**
   - Mobile-first one-thumb control dashboard for ground referees and commentators.
6. **Bilingual Support:**
   - Instant Malayalam (`മലയാളം`) and English toggle for local and global audiences.
7. **Sponsor Showcase:**
   - High-visibility banner space for tournament patrons and local businesses.
